# Pink Prison ("The Pink Prison Cell Experiment") — frame-accurate style analysis

Source: `reference/source/pink-prison.mp4`, 720x1280, 30 fps, 1957 frames (f0-f1956), 65.2 s.
Evidence: all 33 10fps sheets (`reference/sheets/pink-prison/10fps/`), 19 30fps/15fps sheets (`.../30fps/`), 20 stills (`.../stills/`), plus two pixel measurements run over every frame (caption-region change detection; frame-difference for holds). Frame numbers are source frames (t = f/30). Caption start frames between f166-f246, f417-f455, f560-f590 and f1146-f1250 are accurate to about +/-3 frames (bright moving backgrounds confuse the detector; read from 10fps strips); elsewhere +/-1 frame.

Headline: **there is not a single hard cut in the video.** 20 beats are joined by 10 short cross-dissolves (7-12 frames), 1 whip pan, 1 push-into-monitor, 1 fly-through-the-roof, and otherwise continuous camera travel. The "cut" ffmpeg flagged at ~38.9 s is the camera rising through the roof surface (f1165-f1169).

## 2. Camera

**Is the camera ever still? No.** Frame differencing above the caption band (180x200 px proxy): only 4 of 1956 frame pairs have <0.5% pixels changed, and the longest run with <2% pixels changed is 6 frames (f1434-1439, 47.8 s). The calmest stretches are slow creeps, not locks: f756-787 (25.2-26.2 s, low-angle inmate before the stomp), f930-965 (31-32 s), f1080-1110 (36-37 s), f1410-1440 (47 s), f1680-1710 (56 s). Longest uninterrupted *setup* (one framing, slow push): beat 12, f918-1145 = 7.6 s. Typical setup is 1.5-3.5 s.

Other camera traits:
- **Starts already moving at f0** (no fade-in, first caption on f0) and ends mid-move/mid-FX at f1956 (no fade-out).
- **Lens feel**: wide (est. 18-28 mm equivalent) for interiors — strong converging verticals at low angles (stills s_0.70, s_27.50), stretched corners after the CRT push (f1396-1400). Close-ups are just the same wide lens pushed close (no telephoto compression seen).
- **Angles**: low 3/4 looking up into room corners is the default hero angle (beats 1, 9, 10, 20); high CCTV angle for surveillance (beat 15); top-down twice (bucket, crane-up).
- **Roll/dutch**: present during fast travelling moves only (aerial bank, night descent, f1332-1345). Static-ish beats are level. No handheld noise/jitter; motion is smooth spline-like. Small shake only at the bench rip (f1545-1550) — not determinable whether shake or just fast pull-back.
- **Easing**: fast moves accelerate out of the slow drift and ease-out into the next slow drift; never stop dead. Dissolves happen while both shots are in motion.

## 3. Motion & micro-interactions

- **Character animation is pose-to-pose with holds, not interpolated.** At 30fps (sheet t000.00) the guard holds a pose for 2-7 frames and then pops to the next (e.g. roller identical f10-f16, new pose f17, f19, f21, f24-25, f30). The camera and environment update every frame, so characters read as "on twos/threes/variable" inside a 30 fps world. In-betweens are rare; fast actions are 1-2 smear-free pops.
- **Expressions pop** in a single frame (f459, f1452-1458 neutral->shocked circle eyes, f1899 smile->neutral, f1917 ->angry, f1935 ->evil). Blinks: 2-3 frame closed-eye drawings (f117-119, f1380-1386).
- **Mouths**: no real lip sync (narrator voice-over; characters do not speak the lines). Mouth shapes change with the pose: flat line, small "o", wavy line, open smile with teeth, gritted-teeth grid.
- **Walk cycles**: bouncy 2-3 pose walk with noodle legs bending (prisoner f490-530; guards frog-marching f664-700), stepping every ~4-5 frames.
- **Squash/stretch**: mild — head/body stretches on the stand-up at f1929-1933; big inmate torso widens in close-up. No heavy rubber-hose smears observed.
- **Anticipation/overshoot**: arm raises overshoot then settle one pose later (f1930-1934 flex; roller swing f24-f45).
- **Idle**: characters are never frozen for long — head tilts, arm repositioning every 8-15 frames (scientist head scratch f1611-1640; guard nodding off f1380-1390). Knitting needles alternate 2 poses every ~4-6 frames (f1724-1900).
- **Prop animation**: door slam/open in 2-4 frames (f533-535, f541-544, f867-870) with no easing; paint pour stream and swirl animated texture (f180-240), colour of swirl shifts red->pink over ~6 frames (f234-240); stopwatch enters on a noodle arm sliding in from the frame edge (f1803-1830); paint stripe grows with the roller (f17-f30).
- **Background life**: dust motes inside light shafts (s_0.70); floating paint flakes/confetti rectangles drifting and tumbling for ~3 s after the bench rip (f1546-1715); haze in dayroom; clouds with moving shadows in aerial. No light flicker observed except the door-open flash (f1509-1512, ~4 frames to full white bloom).
- **FX vocabulary**:
  - Floor crack decal: radial dark-red/black crack lines grow outward over ~8 frames from the feet (f762-770; f1938-1945) plus small grey debris chips with outlines.
  - Wall "rage veins": jagged glowing red lightning-like cracks grow from the character across walls (f795-850, f1938-1956), redrawn/boiling every 1-2 frames, with red glow halo; a faint circular shockwave ring expands (f1938-1941).
  - Radial burst: pale star/sunburst shapes painted onto walls and ceiling behind the character for ~12 frames (f1546-1558).
  - Impact ticks: short dashes arranged in an arc on the floor (f1515-1535).
  - Debris: outlined concrete cubes and chips flung out, plus persistent rubble.
  - Shadow gag: huge soft character shadow projected on the wall when door light hits (f1512-1545).
  - Evil mode: face gets a dark grey gradient over the top half, red eyes, sharp brows; scene gets a dark magenta vignette.
  - No speed lines, no white/black impact frames, no screen-space smears observed.

## 4. Characters

- **Construction**: stick/"bean" figures. Perfect-circle (slightly oval) off-white head; pill/bean torso; limbs are single uniform-width black strokes (noodle limbs with soft elbows/knees); hands are small black balls (no fingers; a thumb nub appears when gripping); feet are short angled ticks or no feet at all (legs end as line ends).
- **Proportions (measured from stills, head height = 1)**: standard guard/scientist: head 1 : torso ~1.1-1.2 : legs ~1.6-1.8, total ~3.7-4.0 heads; torso width ~0.6-0.7 head. Big inmate: torso ~1.1 heads tall and ~1.2-1.3 heads wide (wider than head), legs ~1.6, total ~3.7. Prisoner: torso ~1.2 heads, narrow (0.5), hair adds ~0.35 head. Head is ~25-30% of total height.
- **Face**: features only — no nose, no ears. Eyes are small black ovals with flat upper lids (half-lidded), or open circles with dot pupils for shock, or upward arcs when happy; brows are thick tapered strokes; optional moustache; blush ovals on happy inmate. Expressions seen: bored/half-lidded, smug smirk, angry gritted teeth, shocked circle-eyes, laughing open mouth, closed-eye bliss with blush, dozing, evil grin with red eyes, wavy-mouth unease.
- **2D or 3D?** Faces and line work look hand-drawn 2D, but the figures hold up under extreme camera angles: top-down at f1160-1167 shows cap tops and heads as foreshortened volumes (still `skylight_f1165-1168.jpg`), low angles keep correct perspective, and they cast correctly projected shadows onto floor/walls (f456-565, f1512-1545). Consistent with 3D toon-shaded bodies (sphere head, capsule torso, tube limbs) with 2D face decals and an outline shell; pure camera-facing billboards would not show cap tops from above. Exact technique not determinable from frames.
- **Turning**: no smooth turnarounds; characters flip between front, 3/4 and profile poses in one frame (guard f24-f30), consistent with swapping face/pose sets.
- **Shading vs the world**: characters are near-flat: head has a very soft radial/gradient shade (lighter centre, greyer edge, #fffefa -> ~#c8c8d0) and receives the scene's colour cast (pinkish #f3d9de in the pink cell, bluish #9ea9da at night/in shadow, s_55.50). They receive broad cast shadows (scientist half-shadowed f456-470) but have no specular or textured detail. The world is fully lit 3D with soft shadows.
- **Costume/prop identifiers**: guards — navy peaked cap with gold star, navy uniform #344e73, gold belt buckle, star badge; one has a moustache. Scientist — brown side-swept hair #4d291c, bare white torso with orange-brown striped tie #b7774c/#9c5d33. Prisoner — big curly orange-red hair, blue-grey jumpsuit with buttons and "4417" tag. Big inmate — bald with stubble dots, white ribbed tank top, blue shorts, anchor tattoos on temple and shoulder.
- **Sampled palette**: outline #301b25 (dark plum, not pure black); head #fffefa; pink-lit head #f3d9de; blush #f0bec9; hair brown #4d291c; uniform navy #344e73; tie #b7774c; scarf #eb73b1 / #efd9e5; needles #eeda6a.

## 5. Outlines / ink

- **Characters**: uniform-width dark plum (#301b25) outline, round caps, no taper on body/limbs. Width scales with distance (world-space, not constant screen-space): ~5-6 px at a full-figure shot where head is ~150 px (s_0.70), ~12-14 px in close-up where head is ~380 px (s_10.50) -> about 3.5-4% of head diameter. Limb stroke equals outline width x ~1.5 (8-9 px at full figure).
- **Inner lines**: thinner (~40-50% of outline) for face features, tie stripes, shirt ribbing, buttons, hair curls; brows are thick and tapered at one end.
- **Hand props** (stopwatch, swatch, roller, needles, paint can): thin 1.5-3 px dark outline, more rendered/gradient fill than the characters.
- **Environment**: no silhouette outlines on walls/furniture. Instead, drawn-on detail lines: brick mortar lines 1-2 px in a darker shade of the wall colour (#e1239d on #e926a1), tile seams, floor expansion joints. Some architectural edges carry a thin darker edge line (door frames, bench edges, railing) giving a "inked 3D" look. FX debris chunks and cracks do have 2-3 px outlines.

## 6. World / environment & textures

- **Modelling**: real low-to-mid-poly 3D sets with correct perspective and parallax: cell (4 walls, ceiling, barred window with deep reveal, bolted steel bench, floor drain, caged tube light, conduit + junction box), two-tier POD C cell block (mezzanine with railings, numbered doors C-1..C-10, roof trusses, pendant lamps, skylights), paint shop, guard booth, prison exterior + farmland.
- **Textures**: flat colour + hand-drawn-looking line maps. Block walls are a tiling running-bond brick pattern (bricks ~2:1, ~85 px wide at medium distance) with slightly uneven, hand-inked mortar lines and subtle per-brick value variation; no photographic textures, no normal-map grit. Floors are flat with a few joint lines. Wood table has plank lines and simple grain strokes. Roof gravel is a voronoi-like pebble pattern (f1168-1170). Farmland is a patchwork of flat coloured polygons with instanced tree dots and cartoon puff clouds.
- **Level of detail**: surprisingly high set dressing at story points: cutting mat with grid, digital scale, labelled cans ("WHITE"), "PAINT SHOP" sign, shelves of coloured cans, drop cloth and roller tray, tally marks, 1979 June calendar, "COFFEE" sticky note, shift roster board, dartboard/fan, key rack, CRT with scanline image, desk lamp, mug, pink box truck with "P-618" livery, traffic cones, watchtower.
- **Signage/text props**: "POD C", door plates "C-4", poster "P-618 BAKER-MILLER PINK / 1 PINT RED + 1 GALLON WHITE / EXACTLY!", swatch "P-618", table label strip "P-618 = 1 PT RED + 1 GAL WHITE", "PAINT SHOP", "DOCK 1"-style plate (not legible), inmate tag "4417", calendar "1979".

## 7. Lighting & glow

- **Key**: motivated by the window or ceiling fixture in each set; usually high and from behind/side of subject, producing long raked shadows toward camera. Dayroom key comes through skylights/windows from upper back-right, throwing a hard-edged window-grid shadow pattern on the floor (s_33.50).
- **Shadows**: world shadows are fairly crisp with slightly soft edges, tinted (blue #8bb3cc on the light floor #dae5e4; deep teal #154a71 on cyan tile #78d5e8), never black. Character cast shadows on the floor are soft blobs/long shapes.
- **Volumetrics**: visible god-ray shafts from every barred window — striped by the bars, with dust motes (s_0.70 shaft #969f9c over #376270 wall); soft vertical shafts in the pink cell (f1398-1500); broad haze beams in the dayroom (f930-1140).
- **Bloom**: strong on windows (blown to #fffeff with ~20-40 px halo), ceiling tube lights, wet paint where the light shaft hits it (#fff2ff core, magenta halo, f20-f60), the pink doorway (#f2b9eb spill onto corridor floor and walls), the CRT and desk lamp at night, and the red FX veins. Door-open flash blooms to near-white over 4 frames.
- **Rim/bounce**: pink cells bounce pink onto characters and onto the grey corridor (f1560-1715). No explicit rim-light on characters.
- **Grade by scene**: opening cell desaturated teal (#376270 walls, #2d444a ceiling); paint shop neutral warm; wrong-shade cell saturated magenta (#e926a1); "right" pink softer (#f185b6 walls, #cd5389 ceiling, floor grey #95959a); dayroom cool pale blue (#88a9bc walls, #a7e0f3 cyan tile, #8cabd5 table); aerial saturated greens (#3ed449, #2eb752, yellow #f5e183, orange #f8b77e, river #4acdc9, haze #acc5c9); night scene deep indigo (#00002e) with warm practicals (desk #f66a61, mat #93fc9f); rage moments add dark magenta vignette (#721b46 corners, crack red #a3102e).
- **Vignette**: mild darkening of corners in most interiors; heavy only in rage beats (beats 10, 20).
- **Time of day**: day throughout except beat 14 (night, crossfaded in over 10 frames).

## 8. Depth

- **Depth of field**: mostly deep focus. Shallow DOF is used selectively: background softened in the paint-shop wide (f117-150), foreground desk edge/glass blurred in the booth shot (f1350-1390), background soft behind the arm-wrestle. Blur is mild (est. 2-5 px at 720p). Foreground blurred objects: desk front edge, door jamb during push-throughs, the drain/vent at bottom of frame in low-angle shots (not blurred, just large).
- **Parallax**: true 3D parallax in every beat — foreground characters/props, mid subject, far wall/mezzanine separate clearly during pushes.
- **Atmosphere**: light blue-white haze in the dayroom (far tier ~30% washed out), aerial perspective fog toward horizon in the flyover, pink fog/glow in cell doorways.
- **Lens distortion**: wide-angle perspective stretch; barrel/CRT warp only during the monitor push (f1392-1398). No chromatic aberration determinable at this bitrate.
- **Motion blur**: present on camera motion during fast moves (whip f66-80, crane/aerial f1160-1245, night descent), absent on character pose pops (they stay crisp).

## 9. Captions

- **Look**: heavy rounded geometric sans (ExtraBold/Black weight, Poppins/Montserrat-like; exact font not determinable), sentence case exactly as spoken (capitals only at sentence starts and "Barbie's"), white fill #ffffff, black stroke ~5-6 px, plus a soft black drop shadow/glow ~3-4 px offset down. No background box.
- **Position**: single line, horizontally centred, baseline band y=882-925 px -> centre at ~70% of frame height (fixed for the whole video; never moves).
- **Size**: x-height+ascender fill ~35 px tall (2.7% of frame height; ~44 px including descenders). Widths: "the" 80 px (11% of width), "scientist" 215 px (30%), "the whole thing" 386 px (54%, the widest card).
- **Words per card**: 178 cards for 237 words -> 1.33 words/card. 126 one-word cards, 45 two-word, 7 three-word. Numbers as digits ("15", "2", "300 lbs", "At 20,").
